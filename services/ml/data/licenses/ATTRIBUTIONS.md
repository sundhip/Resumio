# Open Taxonomy Licenses & Attributions

This directory documents the open source taxonomies and dataset sources utilized to seed and calibrate the **Resumio Skill Ontology and Extraction Engine (Phase 4)**.

---

## 1. ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations)
- **Source:** European Commission Directorate-General for Employment, Social Affairs and Inclusion
- **URL:** https://esco.ec.europa.eu/
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  - https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Attribution Notice:**
  > "© European Union, 1995-2026. Resumio utilizes standardized skill concepts, aliases, and occupational hierarchy structures derived from the ESCO v1.1 classification under CC BY 4.0."
- **Scope Used:** Multilingual technical and soft skill terminology, occupational hierarchies, and canonical skill identifiers.
